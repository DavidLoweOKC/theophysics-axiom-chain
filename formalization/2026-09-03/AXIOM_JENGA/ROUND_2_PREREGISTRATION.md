# Axiom Jenga — Round 2 Preregistration

## Question

Does Round 1 establish a globally unique primitive basis, or can a stronger
alternative rule registry recover the chain after removing a visible primitive?

## Replacement candidates

1. Love derives Truth.
2. Personhood derives Agency.
3. Truth derives Moral Order.
4. Moral Order plus Agency produces a Real Violation.
5. Grace no longer visibly requires External Source.
6. Peace no longer visibly requires Binding Dynamics.
7. Joy no longer visibly requires Positive Mass.

## Mandatory interpretation

A successful replacement is only a formal countermodel to **global uniqueness**.
It is not evidence that the stronger bridge is true.  Candidates 5–7 are
deliberately phrased as hidden-dependency controls: removing a visible input by
baking its work into a stronger conclusion is packaging, not a demonstrated
elimination of the underlying semantic requirement.

Candidate 4 is an aggressive negative control.  Moral order and agency do not
by themselves establish that an actual violation occurred.  If the computation
passes, it demonstrates the consequence of that added bridge, not its adequacy.

## Kill condition

If any smaller visible basis succeeds under a modified registry, the phrase
"the unique minimum basis" must remain explicitly restricted to the original
rule registry and candidate universe.
