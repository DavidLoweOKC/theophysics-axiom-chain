## Results

### Order

Forward and reverse presentation of the five bridge rules reach the identical
final closure after six iterations.  The listed order is explanatory and may
describe dependency depth, but the implementation order is not uniquely fixed.

### Fusion

One opaque fused function reaches all targets from the full basis.  Therefore
the number of functions is packaging.  However, after removing Positive Mass:

- the articulated chain loses only Joy;
- the fused rule loses every derived target.

Intermediate roles are not necessary for final success, but they preserve the
causal/deductive damage map.  Fusion compresses execution while erasing
explanation.

### Labels

A full swap of Love and Positive Mass, conjugated through the graph, preserves
abstract success—as any graph isomorphism should.  More seriously, the unchanged
target set also remains covered because successful closure contains the relevant
nodes regardless of their shuffled semantic role.

This kills the claim that closure success by itself validates these theological
labels.  A semantic adequacy layer must test properties of the nodes and bridges,
not merely membership in a saturated final set.

## Defensible conclusion

> The generator verifies a dependency architecture and its ablation profile.
> It does not determine a unique evaluation order, a necessary number of
> functions, or the correctness of the theological labels.  Articulated
> intermediate stages are valuable because they preserve localized explanatory
> failures that an extensionally successful fused rule erases.  Semantic label
> adequacy remains an explicit open requirement.

## Next construction

The next version needs typed semantic contracts.  Examples:

- Love must preserve distinction/personhood rather than merely occupy a node.
- Truth must satisfy a truth-directed constraint not shared by Positive Mass.
- Justice and Mercy must carry the cost-bearer parameter and conservation law.
- Peace must contain an equilibrium/stability certificate.
- Joy must contain a real-normal-mode or positive-frequency certificate.

A shuffle should then fail because the types and required witnesses no longer
fit, while the audit must still avoid calling a compiler mismatch a physical or
theological necessity proof.
