# Axiom Jenga — Round 6 Preregistration

## Question

Can the Justice/Mercy architecture be reconstructed without defining Justice and
Mercy as incompatible alpha endpoints or as identical predicates?

## Proposed distinction

- Justice concerns truthful adjudication, restoration, and full conservation of
  the cost.
- Alpha controls who bears that conserved cost.
- Mercy concerns voluntary full cost-bearing by another.

## Expected relationship

Mercy should presuppose that justice is fulfilled, but justice fulfillment should
not logically force mercy.  A full-other-payment ledger should permit both
justice fulfillment and mercy, while an offender-payment ledger should witness
justice without mercy.

## Boundaries

- The ledger type enforces accounting conservation by construction; Lean does
  not prove that moral reality has this ledger structure.
- The example uses `Bool` persons and trivially true relational predicates.  It
  is a consistency witness, not a uniqueness or theological identity proof.
- Judge-is-payer, debtlessness, consent, historical Christology, and external
  settlement remain outside this round.
