# Divine Order Kernel — Semantic Checkpoint

No theological identification or chain theorem has been asserted yet.

## Equilibrium structure

`LocalPotentialData` records radius, value, first derivative, second derivative,
and reduced mass. `StableEquilibrium` requires a vanishing first derivative and
strictly positive curvature. `RealNormalMode` separately witnesses a nonnegative
strictly positive real frequency whose square equals curvature divided by reduced
mass. Strict positivity is necessary because zero curvature still permits the
real number `ω = 0` but does not provide a stable oscillation.

Questions for semantic review:

1. Is stationarity plus positive curvature adequate for the proposed Peace
   structure, or must reconciliation/history also be represented?
2. Is a real normal mode adequate for the mathematical component of Joy, while
   conscious participation remains a separate theological bridge?
3. Must Love be represented by the existence of the bound state, its sustaining
   interaction, or an additional relational orientation?

## Violation ledger

`ResponseLedger` permits `alpha > 1`, so Vengeance remains representable.
`ViolationLedger` restricts the restorative interval to `0 ≤ alpha ≤ 1` and
contains distinct offender/injured-party roles, judge, positive cost, both payment
amounts, conservation of the full cost, and an explicit other payer.
`RepairContext` supplies truth-naming, impartiality, injured-party restoration,
authority, and voluntariness predicates. Justice and Mercy candidates share the
adequate-restoration requirements and differ at the cost-bearing endpoint.
`CostBearingJudge` requires the other payer to be the authorized, voluntary judge.
It does not claim uniqueness or universal scope.

Questions for semantic review:

1. `alpha` is currently the fraction of cost borne by the offender. Confirm this
   rather than treating it as punishment intensity.
2. Must restoration of the injured party be a separate required field?
3. Is payment conservation sufficient, or must the ledger distinguish transfer,
   absorption, restitution, and punishment?
4. What prevents an unauthorized third party from satisfying the Mercy candidate?

## Existing-proof boundary

`PrivativeMagnitude.lean` does not formalize Gödel, Tarski, Turing, Clausius, or
Landauer. `KernelV1.lean` proves the bare-Cornell obstruction and proves that a
nonzero two-sector operator cannot be both anticommuting and idempotent. Those are
the only directly reusable results identified for this checkpoint.

## Required semantic corrections

- The bare Cornell potential has no positive-radius stationary point for positive
  coefficients, so its stable-minimum bridge is presently blocked.
- “Frequency is real iff curvature is positive” needs strict positive-frequency
  language. At zero curvature, `ω = 0` is real but not oscillatory.
- Local equilibrium does not prove that an isolated broken system cannot reach it.
  Grace-dependent reachability needs a separate dynamical system and invariant.
