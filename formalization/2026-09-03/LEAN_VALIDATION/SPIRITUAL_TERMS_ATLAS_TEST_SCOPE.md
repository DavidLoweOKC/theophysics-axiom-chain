# Spiritual Terms Atlas — Lean Test Scope

## What this test establishes

- Given six explicitly declared bridges, Love entails Joy through the proposed
  Love → Truth → Justice → Mercy → Grace → Peace → Joy spine.
- The shortened Love → … → Peace consequence also follows.
- Without a Love → Truth bridge, Love alone does not entail Truth in arbitrary
  propositional interpretations; Lean checks a concrete countermodel.
- The bare Cornell stationary polynomial cannot vanish at positive radius for
  positive coefficients. It therefore cannot supply the claimed finite equilibrium
  without an additional stabilizing term or a corrected parent model.

## What this test does not establish

- Lean does not prove that the six bridges are true theology, true physics, or the
  unique meanings of the seven terms.
- It does not upgrade structural correspondences to physical identities.
- It does not classify all terms in the Spiritual Terms Atlas automatically.

## Promotion rule

Each future Atlas claim receives a typed proposition, explicit premises, a theorem
or countermodel, an interpretation status, and a build receipt. No theorem name or
successful compile may silently promote a bridge claim.
